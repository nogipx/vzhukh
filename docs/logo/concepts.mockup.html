vzhukh — logo concepts (sizes: 128 / 64 / 32 / 16)

A. Prompt-swoosh

SSH chevron ">" with speed lines. Reads as terminal + motion. Most legible at small sizes, easy monochrome silhouette.

B. Tunnel

Nested arches with light at the end. Literal "tunnel", calm and trustworthy, but close to many generic network-tool icons.

C. Portal-comet

A comet flies through a ring: the "vzhukh" magic moment, traffic teleported elsewhere. Brightest and most emotional.

D. Route

Your device to your server through hops. Reflects multi-hop routes and sharing; slightly abstract.

E. Vzhukh cat

The meme cat with a sparkle. Funniest and most memorable, but the meme ages and the silhouette is weak for status icons.

A — variations (icon 128 / 64 / 32 / 16, then mono: notification bar + Android themed)

A1. Terminal green

Original: dark graphite, phosphor green. Hacker/SSH mood.

A2. Magic indigo + comet trails

Gradient indigo-violet, white chevron, tapered trails. More "vzhukh" magic, less terminal.

A3. Prompt ">_" on light

Off-white, ink chevron, orange cursor. Most explicit SSH reference, stands out among dark network-tool icons.

A4. Streamlined arrow

Speed lines become the chevron arms: one continuous stroke. Most minimal and logo-like, deep teal + cyan.
